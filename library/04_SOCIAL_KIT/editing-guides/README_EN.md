# Edit DoLaby social templates

Open `../editor.html`. Choose a template ID, Arabic or English, and empty or filled content. Click a text layer to edit. The source separates copy, SVG art, layout and shared styles; previews are exports, not editing masters.

1. Edit copy in the browser, or edit one item in `../master/templates.json` and its matching `templates.js` source. Keep actual product limits and the no-buy outcome honest.
2. Edit original artwork in `../artwork` or use permitted imagery. The shared logo and six Laby pose references remain canonical; do not recolor or distort the black ring.
3. Turn on the crop guide and inspect both languages. Save the edited HTML beside `editor.html` in `04_SOCIAL_KIT`. Saved copies have portable relative asset links and keep text editable; the template-switching toolbar is removed. Reopen `editor.html` to select another template.

`copy-library.csv` contains 48 base-template language rows and 24 sequence-slide rows, including captions and text alternatives. Native story polls/questions must be added in the publishing platform.

No native Canva template was created. The reconstruction map records layer roles and the gap. Current Instagram help could not be verified; see platform-verification.md. TikTok ad dimensions were checked only within the stated surface and do not certify every publishing context. No content was published.
