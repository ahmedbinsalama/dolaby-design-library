## Working crop policy

The page overlay uses72px inset for feed compositions. Vertical guides use200px top,340px bottom,110px left and160px right; these are DESIGN_RESOLVED conservative internal margins, not official universal safe zones. Feed thumbnails and device overlays may crop differently. Keep faces, key clothes, title and call to action in the central content region. Test both Arabic and English copies in the actual destination composer; no content is published as part of this delivery.

The safe guide is separately toggleable and is hidden in clean exports. It is not baked into artwork. Long-copy checks and exported-preview inspection are recorded separately by the production QA pass.
